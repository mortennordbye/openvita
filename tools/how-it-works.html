How it works

The workbench builds and refines CVs from your OpenSelf profile, together with an assistant such as Claude Code if you use one. Everything you do here is stored in files on your machine, and the assistant reads the same files.

Templates and content

A template decides only how the CV looks. The content (the text, the jobs, the certifications) is in cv.html and is the same in every template. Switching template changes the look, not the text. A company template can also bring its logo, work email and office address.

TemplateLookUse it when
ClassicOne column, black on white.The CV goes through an applicant tracking system, or gets printed. The default.
SidebarA dark side column on page 1 with skills and links, a timeline from page 2.You want a more designed look.
MinimalOne column, plenty of white space, one accent colour.You want it calm and short.

Pick a template with the Template button. It shows your own CV in each template, so you see the difference before you switch. The previous version is saved as a revision every time. Your own or your company's templates show up in the same list; see TEMPLATES.md for how to make one.

Using it

  1. Draft from profile builds the CV straight from your profile. It only replaces a CV nobody has edited since its last draft, unless you confirm.
  2. Press New next to Applications, name the folder (yyyy-mm-company-role) and paste the job ad.
  3. Check the ad under Job ad, then press Tailor CV. A dialog says what will happen, whether an assistant is connected, and warns if the ad looks empty. Confirm, and the current CV is saved as a revision before the assistant rewrites it; the page updates when it is done.
  4. Before the interview or meeting, press Prepare, say what kind of meeting it is and pick the parts you want. The assistant writes them under Prep, each point citing the profile entries it comes from.
  5. Read the CV. Select text you want changed, write what should be different and press Send (or Cmd+Enter).
  6. The assistant edits the CV and marks the comment done with a note. See Comments for status.
  7. Happy with it: Save takes a revision. The PDF menu opens the file, or (on macOS) copies it or shows it in Finder.

The chip at the top shows whether an assistant is listening. AI connected (green): new comments and requests reach it within a second, and each open one says Seen by the assistant once it has. No AI connected: they wait. Click the chip to copy the command the assistant should run (make watch-feedback C=<name>), or just ask it to watch the workbench.

You can also select text in the Profile and in the Instructions. A wish about the profile only becomes a change there if you say so, in your own words.

The profile

The Profile view is OpenSelf's own editor (openself/). It holds your whole career, one entry per employment and per assignment. Everything in your CVs comes from here, so what is here must be right. Edit it yourself, or ask the assistant to interview you about an entry.

  • List, Timeline and Technology show the profile from different sides. The dot shows how certain each entry is.
  • Questions and gaps: what is missing or needs an answer. Answer directly, and your words are kept.
  • Edit an entry, New entries, Remove (moved aside with a reason, nothing is deleted).
  • History: every change, yours and the assistant's. Bring back an older version, or see the whole profile as it was on a date.

Flags in an entry: question and conflict stay open until you answer, emphasis and omit are wishes every CV follows, correction is a log.

Rules that do not change

  • Facts in a CV must be in the profile. What is not backed is weakened or removed.
  • Nothing is added to the profile to justify a claim. The assistant writes only what you said.
  • Every change to the profile is its own version, and can be brought back.
  • Feedback is kept as history. You hide it, you do not delete it.
  • Before a CV is changed, a revision is taken, so the previous version can be found.

Good to know

  • Tailoring and comments need an assistant session that follows feedback.json. Ask it to start make watch-feedback C=<name>, or to look at the comments. Without one, requests wait.
  • Lessons in tuning.md are written by the assistant from your comments. Read it now and then, and correct it.
  • The workbench runs only on your machine (127.0.0.1) and stores nothing outside your folders.
